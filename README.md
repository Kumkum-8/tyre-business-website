# TyrePro Business Management Website

A professional wheel and tyre business website with employee login, stock management, customer details, sales tracking, daily profit and expense reports, and OTP-enabled customer service.

## Features
- Employee login dashboard
- Tyre stock overview with quantity and pricing
- Customer records by name, phone, and vehicle
- Sales activity summary
- Daily profit and expense tracking
- Customer service OTP request and verification
- Clean, modern business UI

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.
